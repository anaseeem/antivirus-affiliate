Common questions

Are you Norton or McAfee?

No. MADDY is an independent comparison site. When you click a button here, you go to the official Norton or McAfee website and buy from them directly. Your account, billing and support are all with the vendor.

Do you get paid when I buy?

Sometimes. We may earn a commission from Norton or McAfee if you buy through our links. It doesn't change the price you pay. Read our full disclosure.

Is there a free trial?

Both vendors regularly offer free trials and money-back guarantees. Trial length and terms are set by the vendor, so check the offer page before you sign up.

Why is the renewal price higher?

Both vendors discount the first year and renew at their standard price. You can turn off auto-renew in your vendor account at any time.

I need help with my existing Norton or McAfee subscription.

We can't access your account or provide technical support. Please contact Norton Support or McAfee Support directly.

Do I need antivirus if I have Windows Defender?

Windows' built-in protection covers the basics. Paid suites add things Defender doesn't, like a VPN, password manager, cloud backup, identity monitoring and protection for phones and Macs.

Ready to pick one?

Both offer a money-back guarantee, so you can try one and switch if it isn't right.
